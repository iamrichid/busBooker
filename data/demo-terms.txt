BUS BOOKER TERMS AND CONDITIONS

1. Every booking request is subject to transport desk review and approval.

2. The requester must provide accurate travel dates, times, destination, and passenger count.

3. The bus must only be used for the approved church activity or authorised event listed on the request.

4. Any change to departure time, return time, or destination must be cleared with the transport desk before travel.

5. The requesting group is responsible for keeping the bus clean and reporting any issue, damage, or delay immediately.

6. The bus must return at the agreed time unless a formal extension is approved.

7. Repeated late returns or misuse may affect future booking approval.
